🔧 BIOS Fan Control Guide

Step 1: Enter BIOS

Reboot your system and press DEL or F2 immediately after power-on. You have only a few seconds.

Step 2: Navigate to Thermal Settings

Go to: Power → Thermal Management (or Smart Fan Control)

Step 3: Configure Fan Curves

Use the Balanced Profile (recommended for most users):

CPU Fan:
30°C → 35% | 45°C → 45% | 60°C → 65% | 75°C → 90% | 80°C → 100%

SYS Fan:
30°C → 30% | 45°C → 40% | 60°C → 60% | 75°C → 85% | 80°C → 100%

Step 4: Save and Exit

Press F10, confirm Yes, and let system reboot.

Why BIOS Control?

✓ Safer than Linux tools
✓ More reliable and stable
✓ Firmware handles all thermal logic
✓ No risk of misconfiguration
✓ Works automatically
